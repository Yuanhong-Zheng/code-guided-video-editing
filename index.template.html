THE IDEA

A visible plan.
An editable program.

Video2Code estimates the source scene. Next-code prediction expresses the requested edit as its successor program. Code2Video renders that program into a proxy and uses it to guide video synthesis.

Our training-free prototype jointly realizes reconstruction and program editing in one coding-agent session. The frozen video model receives the source and edited proxy, with proxy guidance limited to the first five steps.
